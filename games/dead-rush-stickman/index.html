Fight through the first ten waves, cash in Camp Points between rounds, and keep the campfire standing for as long as possible.

How To Play

Defend the fire.

WASD / arrows move. Mouse aims. Click shoots. R reloads. Space dashes.

On mobile: left stick moves, right pad aims and fires, side buttons reload and dash.

Zombies attack you and the campfire. Clear the wave, return to the fire, buy upgrades, then start the next round.

If the campfire dies, frenzy mode begins and bosses start dropping in fast until you do too.
